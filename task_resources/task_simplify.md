# Task Explanation: What Are We Building? (As Explained to a 5-Year-Old)

Imagine you and your best friends are building the ultimate secret treehouse club! 🏰✨

To make sure the treehouse gets built properly, you and your friends meet once every single week to talk about how things are going.

We are building a magical computer screen (an app) that helps your team run that weekly meeting and get things done smoothly.

---

### 1. The 4 Magic Toy Boxes

In our treehouse club, we have 4 important lists:

1. **The Scorecard (Measurables) 📏**
   * These are our weekly checkups!
   * *Example:* "Did we collect at least 10 wooden planks this week?"
   * If we collected 12 planks: 🟢 **Green light** (Awesome!).
   * If we only collected 8 planks: 🔴 **Red light** (Uh-oh, problem!).
   * If the week isn't finished yet: 🟡 **Yellow light** (Still working on it!).

2. **The Big Goals (Rocks) 🪨**
   * These are huge things we want to finish before the season ends (like in 3 months).
   * *Example:* "Build a super cool rope ladder."
   * Each big goal has mini-steps (Milestones). As we check off mini-steps, our progress bar fills up!

3. **The Trouble Box (Issues) 🚨**
   * Whenever something goes red on our Scorecard, we can press a magic button: **"Make it an Issue!"**
   * This puts the trouble into our Trouble Box so everyone knows we need to talk about it.
   * We put the most urgent trouble at the top of the box.

4. **The To-Do List (To-Dos) 📝**
   * When we figure out how to solve a trouble, someone promises to fix it!
   * *Example:* "Timmy will ask his dad for 5 extra nails by next Tuesday."
   * Once Timmy brings the nails, he checks it off as done!

---

### 2. The Big Weekly Meeting ⏱️

Every week, the club leader starts the meeting timer. Everyone joins on their own computer screen!

1. **Step 1: Check the Numbers (5 minutes) ⏱️**
   * We look at last week's numbers. If anything is red, we click "Make it an Issue" so it pops into our Trouble Box.
2. **Step 2: Solve the Troubles (60 minutes) 🗣️**
   * We look at the troubles one by one, talk about how to fix them, solve them, and write down new To-Dos with who is responsible.
3. **Step 3: Wrap Up & Star Ratings (10 minutes) ⭐**
   * We look at all the new To-Dos we made, and everyone gives the meeting a score from 1 to 10 stars!
4. **Summary 📜**
   * When the leader ends the meeting, everyone sees a report card showing what got solved and what we promised to do next!

---

### 3. Rules of the Club 🛡️

* **The Captains (Admins & Team Members):** Can change scores, start meetings, fix troubles, and check off tasks.
* **The Observers:** Can look and listen, but cannot touch the buttons or change anything.
* **Everything must stay in sync:** When the leader changes the timer or moves to the next part of the meeting, everyone's screen updates immediately without refreshing!
* **No accidental doubles:** If two people press "Make it an Issue" at the exact same second, only one issue appears!
